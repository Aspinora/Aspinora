How these numbers are calculated
Accuracy = Correct ÷ Attempted × 100 · Attempt Rate = Attempted ÷ Total × 100 · Score % = Marks ÷ Maximum × 100 · Wrong Rate = Wrong ÷ Attempted × 100 · Unattempted = Total − Correct − Wrong · Score Gap = Maximum − Marks · Marks Efficiency = Marks ÷ Attempted. Everything below is computed live from your own result — no peer data, percentiles, ranks, or historical averages are used anywhere on this page.
