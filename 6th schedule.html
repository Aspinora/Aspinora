LADAKH'S DEMAND FOR SIXTH SCHEDULE

"When mountains lose their voice, the Constitution must speak for them. Ladakh seeks not privilege, but survival of fragile ecology, distinctive culture, and tribal identity."

INTRODUCTION

Ladakh's youth-led protests since 2024—hunger strikes by Apex Body Leh and Kargil Democratic Alliance—articulate existential fears post-2019 bifurcation from Jammu & Kashmir. With 97% Scheduled Tribe population, the Union Territory structure stripped legislative autonomy, Public Service Commission, and land safeguards, replacing them with Article 240 regulations modifiable by executive fiat. Demands for Sixth Schedule inclusion reflect anxiety over demographic dilution, ecological despoliation, and cultural erosion in militarised high-altitude desert confronting climate stress and tourism pressures.

BODY: RATIONALE & MECHANISM

Post-2019, Ladakh confronts strategic exposure without local agency: army expansion, solar farms, tourism infrastructure threaten nomadic pastoralism and monastic landscapes comprising 97% tribal landholders. Sixth Schedule offers Autonomous District Councils with legislative powers over land, forests, water, inheritance, and social customs—powers insulating against outsider land grabs and homogenising development. Unlike LAHDC's subordinate status, Sixth Schedule Councils enjoy constitutional entrenchment, elected representation, and veto over parliamentary acts, ensuring self-rule within Union framework.

Triple border vulnerability demands local ecological safeguards

The Sixth Schedule architecture protects tribal identity through elected councils legislating customary law, language preservation, and cultural institutions while devolving governance over local markets, agriculture, and village administration. Governor's oversight ensures constitutional supremacy without nullifying tribal autonomy, creating asymmetric federalism balancing national security with indigenous self-determination.

IMPACTS

Without safeguards, ecological collapse looms: 30% glacier retreat threatens 70% water source; demographic swamping risks cultural extinction; job displacement excludes 97% tribals from development benefits; protest radicalisation fuels alienation as 2024 blockades signal deepening distrust in UT governance.

WAY FORWARD

Constitutional inclusion under Sixth Schedule with robust Autonomous Councils, land-use veto powers, separate PSC, enhanced parliamentary seats offers calibrated solution balancing national security with tribal self-rule. Centre-Ladakh dialogues must culminate in constitutional entrenchment preventing executive overreach while preserving strategic integrity.

CONCLUSION

Ladakh embodies India's civilisational covenant with tribal frontiers. Sixth Schedule inclusion transforms tri-junction vulnerability into strength through empowered local stewardship. The republic must honour constitutional asymmetry ensuring high-altitude sentinels thrive as cultural custodians, not marginalised spectators in their homeland. Tribal autonomy strengthens, never weakens, federal union.
