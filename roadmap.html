Preparation roadmap

The perfect roadmap to your success.

A stage-by-stage plan for SSC CGL and IB ACIO — from foundation to final interview. Follow it at your own pace, and revisit any stage whenever you need to.

Tier 1 — Preliminary

Foundation

Weeks 1–3

Get familiar with the exam pattern and syllabus, start a daily current affairs habit, and build your basics in Quant and Reasoning.

Concept Building

Weeks 4–9

Work through General Intelligence & Reasoning, Quantitative Aptitude, English Language, and General Awareness one topic at a time.

Practice

Weeks 10–13

Solve topic-wise question sets and timed drills to build speed and accuracy.

Revision & Mocks

Weeks 14–16

Take full-length mock tests, analyse your mistakes, and revise weak areas before the exam.

Tier 2 — Mains

Advanced Quant & Reasoning

Weeks 1–3

Move to higher-difficulty problems and shortcut techniques for Paper I.

English Language & Comprehension

Weeks 4–5

Strengthen grammar, vocabulary, and comprehension speed for Paper II.

Statistics & General Studies

Weeks 6–7

Cover the optional papers if you're applying for posts that require them.

Computer Knowledge & Final Mocks

Week 8

Revise computer basics and take sectional and full-length mocks for Tier 2.

Tier 1 — Objective Test

Foundation

Weeks 1–3

Understand the IB ACIO pattern, start a daily current affairs habit, and revise your Quant and Reasoning basics.

Concept Building

Weeks 4–8

Cover General Awareness, Quantitative Aptitude, Reasoning, English Language, and Computer Awareness.

Practice

Weeks 9–11

Practice sectional tests and previous years' papers under timed conditions.

Revision & Mocks

Week 12

Take full mocks, log your errors, and do a final revision pass before the exam.

Tier 2 — Descriptive Paper

Essay Writing Practice

Weeks 1–3

Work through Aspinora's essay topics to build structure, clarity, and argument quality.

Explore essay topics →

Precis & Comprehension

Weeks 4–5

Practice summarising passages clearly and within word limits.

Answer Writing

Weeks 6–8

Practice short and long-form answers across Internal Security, Reforms, Technology, and more.

Explore answer topics →

Mock Descriptive Tests

Week 9

Simulate the actual paper under time pressure and review your writing critically.

Interview — Personality Test

Current Affairs Deep-dive

Ongoing

Go beyond headlines — understand the "why" behind major national and security-related events.

DAF & Personal Profile Prep

Weeks 1–2

Prepare thoughtful, honest answers around your application form, education, and background.

Mock Interviews

Weeks 3–4

Practice with mock panels to get comfortable thinking clearly under pressure.

Final Confidence Building

Week before

Revise current affairs, stay calm, and walk in prepared and self-assured.
