YOUTH UNEMPLOYMENT & RADICALISATION

“Idle hands forge either progress or destruction. When educated youth confront blocked futures, extremism offers purpose where society denied opportunity.”

INTRODUCTION

Youth unemployment constitutes radicalisation's most potent incubator. India's 23% youth unemployment rate (15-29 age) amid 7% GDP growth creates frustrated achievers—educated, aspirational, but opportunity-starved. This demographic timebomb transforms economic grievance into ideological mobilization fuel. Extremists exploit status frustration: promising dignity, community, purpose to those denied by meritocratic mirage. Unemployed youth become radicalisation's perfect vector—tech-savvy recruiters, infinite time availability, nothing to lose.

RADICALISATION MECHANISMS

I. Psychological Vulnerabilities

  • Status anxiety: Engineering graduates driving cabs embrace violent belonging offering instant hierarchy
  • Future discounting: Long-term unemployment erodes time preference; immediate gratification via extremism appeals

II. Socio-Economic Push Factors

  • Relative deprivation: Peers in visible prosperity while self stagnates breeds zero-sum resentment
  • Family pressure amid ₹8 lakh avg engineering fees vs ₹20k monthly jobs creates desperation

III. Technological Pull Factors

  • Dark web recruitment: Encrypted platforms target isolated, online 16+ hours unemployed youth
  • Social media algorithms amplify conspiracy echo chambers converting frustration into ideology

SECURITY CONSEQUENCES

  • Urban jihadists: Middle-class graduates funding IED networks through cybercrime
  • Hybrid threats: Tech-literate recruits execute sophisticated lone wolf operations
  • Naxal cyber wings radicalizing urban support networks for rural insurgency

UNEMPLOYMENT-RADICALISATION PIPELINE

Joblessness → Status Frustration → Online Radicalisation → Operational Recruitment → Terror Execution

Demographic threat: 65% population under 35 = massive recruitment pool

WAY FORWARD

  1. Skill Revolution: Vocational training 10cr youth aligned with Industry 4.0 jobs
  2. Entrepreneurship Ecosystem: ₹1 lakh crore startup fund targeting Tier-2/3 cities
  3. Mentorship Networks: Corporate-social partnerships providing purpose, guidance
  4. Cyber De-radicalisation: AI monitoring + counter-narratives targeting at-risk profiles
  5. Universal Stipend: ₹5k monthly youth allowance bridging unemployment gaps

CONCLUSION

Youth unemployment isn't economic statistic but national security emergency. Demographic dividend morphs into demographic bomb when aspirations confront jobless reality. Radicalisation thrives not on poverty alone but educated idleness—graduates seeking meaning in mayhem. India's survival demands urgent skill-job revolution: convert frustrated achievers into nation-builders before extremists convert them into bombers. The choice is binary—employment ecosystems or endless security expenditure.
